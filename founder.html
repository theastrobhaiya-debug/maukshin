About the Founder

A modern approach to ancient wisdom

Shivam Bansal's work brings together his interest in Vedic astrology, numerology, spirituality and practical life guidance with his experience in entrepreneurship, digital marketing, business development and communication.

Through Mauksh, his vision is to make spiritual tools easier to understand and more accessible to people living in a fast-moving modern world.

Rather than treating astrology as something distant or complicated, Mauksh aims to present it in a way that feels personal, clear and relevant to everyday life.

The platform combines traditional systems with modern technology, creating tools that can help people explore their birth charts, planetary influences, numerology and other areas of spiritual self-understanding.

The Mauksh Vision

Making spirituality easier to explore

Mauksh was built around a simple idea: spiritual knowledge should be accessible without losing the depth of the traditions from which it comes.

Technology can make calculations faster, charts easier to explore and information easier to access. But the human element of interpretation, reflection and understanding remains at the heart of the experience.

This balance between ancient knowledge and modern technology is at the centre of the Mauksh vision.
