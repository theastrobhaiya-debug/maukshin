The Vision

Kaaldarpan was created from a simple idea: astrology should not feel complicated, inaccessible, or disconnected from everyday life.

The vision is to combine the depth of traditional Vedic astrology with modern technology and create experiences that are simple, thoughtful and useful.

About Kaaldarpan

Kaaldarpan brings together Panchang calculations, horoscopes and other Vedic astrology tools in one simple experience.

Its calculations are built around traditional Vedic principles while using modern astronomical computation where appropriate.
