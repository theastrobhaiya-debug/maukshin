About the Founder

Bringing ancient wisdom into a modern world

Shivam Bansal is the founder of Mauksh, an initiative focused on making traditional systems of knowledge more accessible through modern technology and thoughtful digital experiences.

His work brings together an interest in Vedic astrology, numerology, spirituality and practical life guidance with entrepreneurship, digital technology, communication and the process of building products for a modern audience.

Through Mauksh and Kaaldarpan, the goal is not simply to present calculations or traditional terminology, but to make these systems easier for people to explore and understand in the context of their own lives.

Kaaldarpan represents this vision through tools such as Panchang, astrological calculations, planetary information and daily guidance — presented through a simple and accessible digital experience.

The philosophy is simple: technology should make ancient knowledge easier to access without taking away the depth, tradition and personal nature of the journey.

The Mauksh Vision

Ancient knowledge, thoughtfully presented

Mauksh was built around the belief that spiritual knowledge should be approachable without becoming superficial.

Technology can make complex calculations faster, information easier to access and traditional tools simpler to explore. At the same time, astrology and spiritual self-understanding remain deeply personal.

The purpose of Mauksh is therefore to create a bridge between the depth of traditional knowledge and the simplicity expected from modern digital products.

Kaaldarpan is one expression of that vision — bringing Panchang and Vedic astrological information into an experience that is clear, elegant and useful in everyday life.
